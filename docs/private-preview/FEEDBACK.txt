PROGRESSIVE FRICTION — FEEDBACK

Send this back to the person who shared the extension. Nothing here is submitted automatically. You can skip any question and do not need to name sensitive websites.

1. Roughly how many days did you use it? Which operating system and Chrome version?

2. Did restricted websites reliably show a challenge? Did access stay unlocked on refresh and end after leaving the website?

3. Which levels and puzzle types did you try (maths, memory, route planning, logical ordering)? No need to reach every level. Did any become automatic after repeated use?

4. Did the challenge make you reconsider opening the website, or did it just feel annoying? Describe one example if you can.

5. Were any instructions confusing? Was the challenge too easy, too hard, or about right?

6. Any small-window layout problems, unreadable text, distracting motion or keyboard difficulties?

7. Did you disable a restriction or the extension? What made you do so?

8. Any errors or unexpected unlock/count behavior? What happened, what did you expect, and can you repeat it?

9. What ONE change would make you more likely to keep using it?

10. Would you keep using it after this test? Why or why not?
